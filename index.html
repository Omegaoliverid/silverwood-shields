The Shield Wall

Two seals. Fifty tiles. Only one way they fit.

The words in the stone

Five names are carved into the stone: the four the seals hold, and the one who holds them in balance. Tap the first letter of a name, then the last.
The wall grinds aside. A narrow stair leads down into the dark: the way to the Crypt of the Founders.

Your tiles

Drag a tile onto a seal, or tap a tile and then tap a square. Drag it back down to the tiles to remove it.
